errno	DELIBERATE INCOMPATIBILITY (shell route; contract 5, 6): /bin and /etc are read-only mounts of the session, so create in /bin and write-open of /etc are EROFS where Linux (no root) says EACCES, and ENOENT for a host without /etc/motd; the cwd-relative O_CREAT|O_EXCL lines and the rest agree
